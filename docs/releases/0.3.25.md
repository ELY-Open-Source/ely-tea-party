本版同步了上游 ELYTerminal 0.2.6：会话可以命名分组，重启后分屏也会恢复。

## 新功能

- **会话分组**：右键会话行可以新建分组、移到某个分组或移出分组；右键分组标题可以重命名或取消分组，双击标题也能重命名。两种侧边栏样式都支持：紧凑样式里，每个分组下面再按文件夹分开。新会话会加入当前会话所在的分组。
- **分屏完整恢复**：重启后，每个会话的分屏布局、各窗格的程序和文件夹、分组都会回来，agent 的对话照旧接着续上。
- **调整分屏**：拖动分屏之间的分隔线可以改变大小；Cmd-Alt-方向键把当前窗格和那一侧的窗格互换（Windows 和 Linux 上是 Ctrl-Shift-Alt-方向键）。
- **拖文件夹到 Dock 图标**：macOS 上把文件夹拖到 Dock 里的 Ely Tea Party 图标上，会在那里开一个新会话。
- **缩头探出**：设置 › 终端 打开“缩头探出”后，Ely 平时只露出头顶和呆毛，指针移过去她才探出来。只对 Ely 有效，鲸鱼娘不变。

## 修复

- macOS 上按 Cmd-Q 退出不再崩溃；空闲的登录 shell 关闭时不再询问。
- 终端最右一列和最下一行的背景色能铺满到边缘。
- 没有选中文字时，复制键不会吞掉 Ctrl-C，程序照样能收到中断。
- 来自上游的其他终端修复。

## 兼容性

- Ely 的表情只跟着当前显示的会话（包括它的 agent），后台会话在等待时她不再着急。等待中的会话仍会在侧边栏和茶会里提醒你。
- 拖动排序只在同一个分组内有效；要换组，请用右键菜单。
- 旧版保存的会话照常恢复。但如果保存时有分组或分屏，退回旧版后读不了这份记录，会以全新会话启动。

## What's new

This release syncs with upstream ELYTerminal 0.2.6: sessions gather in named groups, and splits come back after a restart.

- **Session groups.** A row's right-click menu makes a new group, moves the session to a group or takes it out. A group header's menu renames or ungroups it, and a double click renames it too. Both sidebar styles show groups; in the compact style, each group holds its own folders. A new session joins the shown session's group.
- **Splits come back.** After a restart, each session's split layout, each pane's program and folder, and its group return, and agents still resume their conversations.
- **Resize and move panes.** Drag the line between panes to resize them. Cmd-Alt-arrow swaps the shown pane with its neighbor on that side, or Ctrl-Shift-Alt-arrow on Windows and Linux.
- **Drop a folder on the Dock icon.** On macOS, a folder dropped on Ely Tea Party's Dock icon opens a session there.
- **Peek low.** With Peek low on in Settings › Terminal, Ely shows only her hair and ahoge until the pointer comes over her. It applies to Ely; the whale girl is unchanged.

## Fixes

- On macOS, Cmd-Q quits without a crash, and an idle login shell no longer asks at close.
- The last column's and last row's background colors reach the terminal's edges.
- With nothing selected, the copy key no longer swallows Ctrl-C, so the program still gets its interrupt.
- Upstream's other terminal fixes.

## Compatibility

- Ely's face follows the shown session and its agent only, so a waiting session in the background no longer worries her. Waiting sessions are still flagged in the sidebar and the tea party.
- A drag reorders rows inside their group only; use the right-click menu to change a session's group.
- Sessions saved by older versions come back as before. Once groups or splits are saved, an older version cannot read them and starts with fresh sessions.
